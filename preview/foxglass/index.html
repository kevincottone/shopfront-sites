What we do

Automotive window tint, done with pride

Sedan, SUV, truck or something you've waited years to own. Bring it to the shop on US 19 and it gets measured, cut and laid by hand. Want to watch? Customers say the crew doesn't mind.

Full vehicle tint

All side windows plus the rear glass. Use the sliders above to play with shades, or let John recommend one for how and where you drive.

Front windows to match

SUV or truck already dark in the back from the factory? We match the two fronts so the whole vehicle looks like one piece.

Windshield sun strip

A strip across the top of the windshield to knock down the low-sun glare on your morning and evening drive.

Re-tints and old tint removal

Purple, bubbling or peeling film stripped off clean and the glass prepped, so the new film goes on right.

Whole-family fleets welcome

A lot of Fox customers bring every car in the household, one after another. Getting two done? Mention it when you text and we'll plan the days around you.
