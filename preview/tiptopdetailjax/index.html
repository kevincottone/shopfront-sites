Services

Everything a car needs to look cared for.

One shop handles the whole list: a thorough detail, paint correction and ceramic when you want it to last, window tint to cut the Florida heat, and a wash club so it never slides back.

Signature service

Full detailing

Hand wash, wheels, tires, jambs and glass outside. Vacuum, shampooed seats and carpet, conditioned leather, plastics and every vent inside. The attention-to-detail job the reviews keep mentioning.

Interior detailing

Kid crumbs, pet hair, coffee stains and that smell you've stopped noticing. Deep cleaned down to the seat rails and left smelling like nothing at all.

Exterior detailing

Two-bucket hand wash, iron and tar decontamination, clay bar, dressed tires and a sealant so the shine lasts past the first rain.

Ceramic coating and paint correction

Swirls and water spots polished out first, then a ceramic coating that fights UV and love bugs and makes every wash afterward easier.

Window tinting

Heat-rejecting film for a cooler cabin, less glare and a cleaner look. Shade options to match Florida's legal limits.

Maintenance wash club and mobile detailing

A monthly plan that keeps a detailed car detailed, and mobile service across Jacksonville when the shop is out of your way.
