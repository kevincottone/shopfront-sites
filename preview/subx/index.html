What we do

Tint, installed in your driveway

Every vehicle is cut and installed on site by Paul himself. Pick a shade, or tell him how you drive and let him recommend one.

Full vehicle tint

All side windows plus the rear glass, shade matched all the way around. Sedans, trucks, SUVs and work vans.

Windshield tint

A light, Florida-legal film across the full windshield that knocks down heat and glare without darkening your view of the road.

Front windows to match

Factory privacy glass in back already? Match the two fronts so the whole car reads as one, usually in under an hour.

Old tint removal

Purple, bubbled or peeling film stripped clean, adhesive and all, before the new film goes on.
