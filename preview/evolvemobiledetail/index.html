Services

From a quick wash and wax to a coating that lasts years.

Pick one service or combine them. Wade brings water, power and everything else to your driveway across Jacksonville, St. Johns and Ponte Vedra.

Mobile wash and wax

Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, glass, and a coat of wax for gloss and protection. The right cadence for a car that gets washed often.

Mini detail with steam cleaning

A reset for the interior: steam on seats, carpets, vents and cupholders, plus a wash outside. Less time than a full detail, more than a car wash.

Full detail with steam cleaning

Everything inside and out. Seats and carpet steam cleaned and extracted, leather conditioned, paint clayed and sealed, every crevice reached.

1-step paint enhancement

A single machine polishing pass that removes light swirls and haze and brings the depth back, without the time of a full multi-step correction.

3-year ceramic coating package

Paint enhancement followed by a ceramic coating that locks in the gloss and shrugs off sun, rain spots and love bugs. Washes get easier for years.

Maintenance details

A recurring visit, monthly or every other month, that keeps a detailed or coated car looking the way it did the day it was finished.
