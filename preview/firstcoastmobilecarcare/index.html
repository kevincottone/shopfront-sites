Services

Most jobs in your driveway. The rest in our shop.

The truck carries the scanner, tools and common parts, so brakes, batteries, tune-ups and most diagnostics are finished on site. When a job genuinely needs a lift, we'll say so first and handle it in the shop.

Diagnostics

Check engine, ABS and warning lights, rough running, stalling or a no-start. Scanned and tested where it sits, cause explained before anything is replaced.

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers and lines on cars, trucks and work vans. Grinding, squealing or a soft pedal handled at your place.

Battery and electrical

No crank, slow crank, dimming lights or a battery that keeps dying. Battery, alternator, starter and wiring tested so you only pay for what's actually bad.

Diesel repair

Light and medium-duty diesel pickups and vans. Glow plugs, injectors, fuel system, turbo and emissions issues, with the know-how reviewers mention by name.

Heating and A/C

Blowing warm in August or cold in January. Leak check, recharge, blower motors and component repair so the cabin is right again.

Engine and transmission

Repairs, rebuilds and replacements, including remanufactured engines and transmissions. The heavy work goes into the shop; you get a straight quote first.

Oil changes and maintenance

Oil and filter, fluids, belts, hoses and tune-ups on your schedule. Preventive work that keeps a daily driver or a work truck out of trouble.

Suspension and 4x4

Shocks, struts, control arms, ball joints, hubs and transfer cases. Lifted trucks and off-road rigs welcome.

Fleet services

Keep work vans and trucks on the road. Scheduled maintenance and repairs at your yard so a down vehicle doesn't become a down crew.

Run a fleet in Jacksonville?

We service work vans, pickups and light trucks at your lot, on a schedule that fits your crews. Call (904) 625-3460 to set up fleet maintenance.
