Services

From the daily driver to the RV.

Book one service or stack a few. Every visit is fully mobile, so there's nothing to drop off and nothing to tow.

Full interior and exterior detail

Hand wash, wheels and tires, jambs and glass outside; vacuum, shampoo, wipe-down and dressing inside. The whole car, start to finish.

RV and trailer detailing We come to you

Motorhomes, campers, travel trailers and horse trailers washed and detailed where they're parked. Faded decals brought back to color, black streaks and road film removed.

Headlight restoration

Yellowed, cloudy lenses sanded, polished and sealed until they're clear again. Better looks, better night visibility, and a cheap upgrade before you sell.

Engine bay cleaning

Grease and dust carefully cleaned off and plastics dressed, so it looks right when you pop the hood for a buyer or a mechanic.

Paint transfer and overspray removal

Scuffs from another car, concrete or paint overspray from a job site. The stuff the car wash can't touch, lifted off without hurting your paint.

Trucks and work vehicles

Work trucks, farm trucks and family SUVs welcome. Several vehicles at the same address can be done in one visit.

About

Eddie and his crew, based in Plant City.

A Touch of Class Mobile Detailing is run by Eddie out of Plant City. He and his crew bring everything to you, whether it's a sedan in the driveway, a work truck covered in job-site overspray, or a motorhome that hasn't moved since last season.

Customers say the same things again and again: on time, friendly, honest about what the vehicle needs, and fast to respond. One customer called after three car washes couldn't fix a paint transfer, and Eddie was in their driveway within the hour.
