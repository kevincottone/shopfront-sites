Services

Inside, outside, and the smell you gave up on.

Pick one service or combine them. Nathan comes to your home or office, or you can drop the car at the shop.

Mobile and in-shop detailing

Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, glass, and a full interior clean that reaches every crevice. Done at your place or ours.

Carpet and seat shampoo

Hot-water extraction on seats, carpet and mats to lift stains, spills and ground-in dirt. The interior that looked past saving comes back.

Ozone odor treatment

Smoke, mildew, pet and food smells neutralized at the source with an ozone treatment, not masked with a scent that fades in a week.

Clay bar and paint correction

Clay bar decontamination pulls bonded grit off the paint, then machine polishing removes swirls, haze and water spots for real gloss.

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer that locks in the gloss after correction and shrugs off Florida sun, rain spots and love bugs. Washes get easier for years.
