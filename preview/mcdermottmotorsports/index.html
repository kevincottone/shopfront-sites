Services

What Chris fixes on site

Scanner, tools and common parts ride in the truck, so most jobs are finished the same visit. If something truly needs a lift, you'll hear that first, not after.

Mobile diagnostics

Check engine light, rough idle, stalling or a car that won't start. Scanned and tested in your driveway, with the cause explained before anything is replaced.

Brakes

Pads, rotors and calipers. Grinding, squealing or a pedal that goes soft gets a fast response and a fair, upfront price.

Suspension

Shocks, struts, control arms and bushings. Clunks, drifting and a rough ride sorted without leaving the car at a shop.

Electrical, starters and alternators

Dimming lights, a click and nothing, a battery that keeps dying. The charging system is tested and the bad part swapped on site.

A/C diagnostic and recharge

Blowing warm in a Jacksonville summer is not an option. Leak check, recharge and component repair so it runs cold again.

Oil changes and tune-ups

Oil and filter, spark plugs, belts and fluids. The preventive work that keeps a car out of trouble, done while you're at home or at work.

Pre-purchase inspections

Buying used? Chris meets you at the seller's, scans it, looks underneath and tells you plainly what it needs before you hand over money.

ECU coding

Module coding and programming after a part swap, plus the diagnostic work that goes with it, handled on site.

24-hour roadside

Jump-start, flat tire or a no-start at 2am. Call any hour and get a straight answer about how soon Chris can be there.
