Services

Interior detailing

Seats, carpet and mats shampooed, leather and upholstery cleaned and conditioned, vents, cup holders, door jambs and seals. The kind of clean that makes it feel like a new car again.

Exterior wash and wax

Hand wash, wheels and wheel wells, tires dressed, glass and trim, finished with a hand wax so the paint beads and shines for weeks.
