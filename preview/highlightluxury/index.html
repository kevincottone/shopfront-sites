Services

From a quick refresh to a full correction.

Every job is fully mobile, so there's nothing to drop off and nowhere to wait. Pick a package or ask Hakim what the car actually needs.

Express package

A fast, thorough refresh: hand wash and dry, wheels and tires, a quick interior vacuum and wipe-down, glass inside and out. Ideal between full details.

Modest package

The middle option. Everything in express plus a deeper interior: seats, carpets and mats cleaned, leather conditioned, door jambs, and a sealant on the paint.

Full-service package

The complete inside-and-out detail. Shampooed interior, clay bar and decontamination on the paint, a light polish, and protection on every surface.

Paint correction

Machine polishing that lifts swirls, water spots and light scratches out of the clear coat, so the color looks deep and the reflection is sharp again.

Protection services

Long-term protection for the paint, wheels and glass so the finish shrugs off Florida sun, love bugs and rain, and washes stay quick.

Memberships

A standing visit on a schedule that suits you. Hakim keeps the car looking like it did after the first detail, without you having to book each time.
