Why ceramic in Florida

Heat, not just shade

Ceramic film blocks infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler even at a lighter, legal shade. Cheaper dyed film only darkens the glass.

No signal interference

No metal in the film, so phone, GPS, SunPass and radio all work exactly as before.

Won't turn purple

Dyed film fades to purple in a couple of Tampa summers. Ceramic holds its color, which is why we can put a lifetime warranty on it.
