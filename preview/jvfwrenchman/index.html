Services

Shop-grade work, done at your curb

From a check-engine light to a full engine swap, most of it happens right where the vehicle is parked. If a job truly needs a lift, you'll hear that before anything comes apart.

Engine diagnostics & repair

Scanner on, codes read, the actual cause traced and explained in plain English. Misfires, stalls, overheating and warning lights fixed where they happened.

Engine & transmission replacement

Yes, mobile. Full engine and transmission swaps with a three-day turnaround, so the vehicle is back on the road without a tow or a month at a shop.

Brakes & rotors

Pads, rotors, calipers and hubs, front and rear. Grinding, pulsing or a soft pedal handled in your driveway, usually in one visit.

Steering & suspension

Ball joints, U-joints, CV axles and rack-and-pinion replacements. The clunk, the wobble and the wandering steering sorted at the source.

Tune-ups & tires

Plugs, coils, filters, belts and fluids to keep the big repairs away, plus tire swaps and flats handled on site.

Used vehicle inspections

Buying a car off Marketplace or a lot? Ian meets you there, goes through it top to bottom and tells you what it's really worth before you hand over cash.
