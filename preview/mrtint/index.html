What we do

Automotive window tint, done one car at a time

Every vehicle gets Mike's full attention from the first call to the final wipe-down. Tell him how you drive and he'll recommend a film and shade that fits.

Full vehicle tint

All side windows and the rear glass, cut to fit your vehicle. Cars, trucks, SUVs and Broncos, with the fronts kept Florida-legal.

Failing dealer tint, redone

Bubbling, peeling or purple dealer tint on a newer car? Mike strips it and re-tints the whole vehicle so it finally looks right.

Fronts to match factory

Factory privacy glass in back already? Match the two front windows so the vehicle reads as one, without redoing glass that doesn't need it.

Honest advice first

Not sure what you need? Text Mike a photo. If removing what you have isn't worth it, he'll tell you, even if that means a smaller job.

From the shop: leave your windows rolled up for a few days after install so the film can cure flat to the glass.
