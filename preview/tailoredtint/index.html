What we do

Window tint, done to fit

Every job is measured and cut for your specific vehicle, so the film sits flush to the edge with no light gaps and no peeling corners.

Full vehicle tint

All side windows plus the rear glass. Pick a shade on the slider above or let Parker recommend one for how you drive.

Front windows to match factory

Most newer cars come tinted in the back only. We match the two fronts to the factory rear so the whole car reads as one.

Windshield heat film

A light, Florida-legal film across the windshield that cuts heat and glare without darkening your view of the road.

Old tint removal

Purple, bubbled, or peeling film stripped clean, adhesive and all, before new film goes on.

Customers also come to Parker for vinyl stripes and accents. Ask when you reach out.
