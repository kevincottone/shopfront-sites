Services

Fix the paint first. Then lock it in.

A coating only looks as good as the paint under it, so the work starts with correction. From there it's ceramic for the long haul, or a straight detail that brings a tired car back.

Signature service

Ceramic coating

The paint is washed, clayed and machine polished, then a professional ceramic coating is applied and cured. UV, bird droppings, salt spray and love bugs stop eating the clear coat, and washes take half the time. Multi-year packages available.

Paint correction

Machine polishing that removes swirl marks, water spots, oxidation and light scratches so the paint looks deep again instead of flat.

Interior and exterior detailing

Hand wash, wheels, jambs and glass outside. Shampooed carpet, conditioned leather, plastics and every vent inside. Book one side or the full package.

Mobile detailing

Same products, same standard, in your driveway or office lot across the Jacksonville area. You get your car back without losing the morning.

Boat, RV and aviation detailing

Oxidized gelcoat brought back, hulls and decks sealed, motorhomes washed and waxed, aircraft exteriors cleaned and protected. Quoted by the foot.

Fleet cleaning

Work trucks, vans and dealer inventory on a recurring schedule so every vehicle rolls out looking like the company cares.
