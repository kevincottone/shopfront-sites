What we do

Every film the car needs, under one roof.

Daily drivers, new cars off the lot, and the one you've been saving for. Tell Eric how you want it to look and how long you plan to keep it, and the shop will put together the right package.

Window tint

All sides and rear, or just the fronts to match. Carbon and ceramic films, cut clean with no gaps at the edges, fronts kept Florida-legal.

Paint protection film

Clear bra on the bumper, hood, fenders and mirrors, plus windshield protection film for the rock chips Blanding Boulevard throws at you.

Ceramic coating

A hard, glossy layer over the paint that keeps water beading, makes washing easier and holds the shine through a Florida summer.

Chrome delete

Window trim, grille, badges and mirror caps wrapped in gloss or satin black for a cleaner, blacked-out look.

Headlight & taillight tint

A light smoke on the lamps to match the rest of the car, in a shade that still throws plenty of light at night.

Vinyl wraps

Full color changes, satin and matte finishes, roofs and hoods. A new look that peels away later, leaving the paint untouched.

Home and business glass too. Residential and commercial tint cuts heat and glare in sunrooms, offices and storefronts. Quoted on site.
