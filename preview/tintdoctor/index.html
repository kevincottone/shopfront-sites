What Eddie does

Automotive tint, start to finish

One installer, one specialty. Cars, trucks and SUVs get a quick talk about shade and film first, so you know exactly what you're getting before any glass is touched.

Full vehicle tint

All side windows plus the rear glass, cut to fit your vehicle. Most cars are done in about two hours, so you can wait or grab dinner on US-19.

Two fronts to match

Truck or SUV with factory privacy glass in back? Eddie matches the two front windows so the whole vehicle looks even, and keeps them Florida-legal.

Windshield sun strip

A strip across the top of the windshield to cut the glare when the sun is low over the Gulf on the drive home.

Old tint off, new tint on

Purple, peeling or bubbled film removed and the glass redone. If something lifts after your install, text Eddie and he'll take a look.

Doctor's orders: keep the windows rolled up for the first few days and follow the aftercare instructions. A little haze or a few small bubbles while the film cures is normal.
