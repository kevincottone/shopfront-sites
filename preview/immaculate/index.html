Services

Fix the paint first. Then protect it.

A coating only looks as good as the paint under it, so the work starts with correction. Book the full treatment or just a detail. Samuel comes to your home or office.

Signature service

Paint correction + ceramic coating

The paint is decontaminated and machine-polished to pull swirls, haze and water spots out of the clear coat, then a ceramic coating goes on to hold that gloss. Water beads off, love bugs and bird droppings wipe away, and washing takes half the time.

Paint correction

Machine polishing on its own for swirl marks, light scratches and stubborn water spots. Great before selling, or on a new car the dealer wash already scratched up.

Full interior and exterior detail

Hand wash, wheels, jambs and glass outside. Seats, carpet, plastics and every vent inside. Customers say it's the interior that surprises them most.

Headlight restoration

Yellowed, foggy lenses sanded, polished and sealed so they look new again and actually light the road.

Trim and plastics

Faded black trim and plastics cleaned and brought back to a deep, even finish so they match the corrected paint.
