What we do

Tint by Scott. Detail by Peter.

Two specialties under one roof on Park Blvd. Bring the car in for one and leave with both, or book either on its own.

Window tint

Film for cars, trucks and SUVs, cut to fit your glass and installed clean. Scott walks you through shades, keeps the fronts within Florida law and matches factory privacy glass on the rear.

  • Full vehicle, two fronts or windshield strip
  • Shade guidance and Florida legal limits explained
  • Old, purple or bubbled film removed
  • Built to hold up under the Florida sun

Detailing

Interior and exterior detailing done by hand. Peter gets the sand out of the carpet, the film off the glass and the swirl marks out of the paint so the car looks the way you remember it.

  • Interior deep clean, seats, carpet and vents
  • Hand wash, clay bar and wax or sealant
  • Paint correction for swirls and light scratches
  • Headlight restoration and engine bay cleanup

Doing both? Reviewers keep mentioning the same thing: tint from Scott, detail from Peter, one drop-off. Text the shop and ask about booking them together.
