What we do

Three shops' worth of work under one roof

Most places do one of these. Gus's does all three on East MLK Blvd, so one drop-off can cover the check engine light, the cracked windshield and the tint you've been meaning to get.

01

Auto repair

Maintenance and mechanical repair for everyday cars and trucks. Fair prices, honest answers, and work explained before it starts.

  • Diagnostics and check engine lights
  • Brakes, pads and rotors
  • Alternators, starters and batteries
  • Oil changes and routine maintenance
  • A/C, cooling and belts
02

Auto glass

Cracked windshield, smashed side window or broken back glass. Replaced at the shop so you're back on the road with a clear view.

  • Windshield replacement
  • Side and door window replacement
  • Back glass replacement
  • Chip and crack checks
03

Window tint

Film for cars, trucks and SUVs that cuts the heat and glare of a Plant City summer, with the fronts kept within Florida's legal limits.

  • Full vehicle or two fronts to match
  • Windshield sun strip
  • Old, purple or bubbled tint removed
  • Shade guidance before install

New glass? Tint it before you leave. Getting a side window or back glass replaced is the right time to match the tint. Ask Gus to do both in the same visit.
