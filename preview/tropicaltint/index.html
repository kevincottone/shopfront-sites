What we do

If it has glass, Luis can tint it

Tropical Window Tint handles the whole range: daily drivers, Teslas, the house, the office and the boat. Tell Luis what you've got and he'll recommend a film and shade for how you use it.

Automotive & ceramic tint

All side windows plus the rear glass, with ceramic film for drivers who want the heat gone, not just the glare. Fronts kept Florida-legal.

Tesla computer-cut tint

Patterns cut by machine for Model 3, Y, S and X, including the big rear glass, so the film fits the edges without blades near your trim.

Residential & commercial film

Cut the afternoon heat on west-facing rooms and storefronts, protect furniture from fading, and keep the view out.

Boat tint

Marine film for cabin and console glass that stands up to the Florida sun on the water.

3M clear bra

Paint protection film on the hood, bumper and mirrors so gravel on I-4 doesn't leave its mark.

Wraps, headlights & LED lighting

Car wraps, headlight restoration for yellowed lenses, and LED lighting upgrades, all under one roof.
