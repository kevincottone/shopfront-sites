What we do

Film for every window in the building

From a single sunroom to a whole office. Blake measures, recommends the right film for how you use the space, and installs it cleanly in place.

Heat & glare control

Solar film for living rooms, sunrooms, sliders and west-facing glass. Cuts heat and glare so the AC runs less, with little to no mirroring from inside.

Security & safety film

Thick film that holds shattered glass together on doors, storefronts and ground-floor windows. Slows a break-in and keeps glass out of the room.

Privacy & decorative film

Frosted, etched and patterned film for front doors, bathrooms, kids' rooms and conference rooms. Blocks the view, keeps the light.

Commercial & office

Storefronts, offices, medical suites and warehouses. Quoted by the project and scheduled around your business hours.

Mobile installer. We come to you. Blake measures and quotes on site, usually the same day you call, and installs at your home or office. Customers mention he even helps take down and rehang the blinds.

Pricing

Priced by the window, not the hour

Window film is quoted per square foot of glass. Here is where each option starts; Blake measures on site and gives you one number for the whole job.

Good

Solar film

Dyed or carbon film that cuts heat and glare and blocks UV so floors and furniture don't fade. The everyday choice for most rooms.

Most popular

Ceramic film

Blocks infrared heat without going dark or mirrored, so the room stays bright and the view stays clear. Best for sunrooms and big sliders.

  • Keeps natural light
