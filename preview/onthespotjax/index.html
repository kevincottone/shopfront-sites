What we do

Tint is the start. Protection is the rest.

Window tint is the reason most people find us. Paint protection film, ceramic coating, vinyl wrap and detailing are why they come back. Tell us what you have and what bugs you about it, and we'll walk you through the options.

Automotive window tint

Sedans, trucks, SUVs and vans. Economy film for a clean dark look, carbon for better heat rejection, or ceramic for the most heat cut at any shade.

Ceramic tint

Blocks infrared heat instead of just light, so the cabin stays cooler even at a lighter, Florida-legal shade. No metal, no signal interference.

Paint protection film

Clear film over the hood, bumper, fenders and mirrors, or the whole car. Takes the rock chips and sand on I-95 so your paint doesn't.

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer over the paint that beads water, resists UV and makes washing a five-minute job. Pairs well with PPF.

Vinyl wrap

Color change, gloss black roof, chrome delete or a partial wrap. A new look without touching the factory paint.

Home and commercial tint

Residential and commercial film for sliders, west-facing windows and storefronts. Lower AC bills, less fading, more daytime privacy.

Detailing too. Interior and exterior detail packages, on their own or added to any tint, PPF or coating appointment.
