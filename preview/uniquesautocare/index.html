Services

Fix the paint first. Then protect it.

A coating or film only looks as good as the paint under it, so the work starts with correction. Pick ceramic for gloss and easy washing, PPF for rock chips on the front end, or both.

Signature service

Ceramic coating

The paint is decontaminated and machine-polished, then a ceramic coating goes on to hold the gloss. Water beads off, love bugs and bird droppings wipe away, and the Florida sun stops chalking the clear coat. Washing takes half the time.

Paint protection film

Clear, self-healing film on the hood, bumper, fenders and mirrors, or the whole car. The I-4 gravel hits the film, not your paint.

Paint correction

Machine polishing for swirl marks, light scratches, water spots and rock chips. Customers say you can't tell the chips were ever there.

Headlight restoration

Yellowed, foggy lenses sanded, polished and sealed so they look new again and actually light the road at night.

Odor removal

Smoke, pets, mildew and whatever got spilled in the back seat. Treated at the source, not covered up with a scent.

Auto detailing

Hand wash, wheels, jambs and glass outside. Seats, carpet, plastics and every vent inside. The same care whether it's a daily driver or a weekend car.
