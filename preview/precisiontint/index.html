What we do

Two specialties, one shop.

Bring the car in for a sound system and leave with it tinted too, or the other way around. Lighting and security round it out.

Car audio

From a touchscreen head unit with Apple CarPlay or Android Auto to a full amp-and-sub build. Factory-look installs, clean wiring, tuned before you leave. We also fix installs that someone else got wrong.

  • Head units and touchscreen receivers
  • Speaker upgrades and component sets
  • Amplifiers, subwoofers and custom enclosures
  • Backup cameras and dash cams

Window tint

Ceramic and carbon film for cars, trucks and SUVs. We'll walk you through shades, keep the front windows within Florida law, and match factory privacy glass on the rear.

  • Full car, two fronts or windshield strip
  • Ceramic film for heat, not just shade
  • Old, purple or bubbled tint removed
  • Most cars done in about two hours

Lighting

LED headlight and fog upgrades, light bars and pods on trucks, interior and underglow accents, all wired properly with switches where you want them.

Security

Alarms, remote start so the cabin is cool before you get in, and GPS trackers. Installed to work with your factory key and electronics.
