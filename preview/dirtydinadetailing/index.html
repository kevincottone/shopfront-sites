Services

Cars, trucks, boats and RVs, cleaned where they sit.

Pick one service or combine them. Nic brings water, power and everything else to your home, the dock or the storage lot.

Full, interior or exterior detail

Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, glass, and a deep interior clean that reaches every seam and cupholder. Book the whole thing or just the half you need.

Steam cleaning, mold and mildew

Steam lifts sand, salt and grime out of fabric and seams without soaking the cabin, and it's the right tool when a beach car or closed-up boat has started growing something.

Odor removal

Smoke, mildew, pet and fish smells neutralized at the source, not masked with a scent that fades by the weekend.

Ceramic coating package

Paint polished, then sealed under a hard, slick coating that shrugs off salt air, sun, rain spots and love bugs. Washes get easier for years.

RV and boat detailing

Oxidized gelcoat and fiberglass brought back, hulls and sidewalls washed and sealed, vinyl and interiors cleaned. Done at the dock, the ramp or the storage lot.

Headlight restoration and maintenance plans

Hazy lenses sanded, polished and sealed. Then a recurring maintenance detail, monthly or seasonal, keeps the whole thing from sliding back.

About

Owner-run, based in Fernandina Beach.

Dirty Dina Detailing is run by Nic out of Fernandina Beach. He's the one who answers, shows up, and does the work, from a beach-day SUV full of sand to a pontoon that's been sitting at the dock all summer. Living on the island means he knows what salt air, sun and sandy feet do to an interior, and how to get it back.

Mobile means you don't lose a day. Book it for while you're home or at work, or meet him at the ramp or the storage lot, and come back to a finished vehicle.
