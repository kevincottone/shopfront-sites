Services

From a premium detail to a mirror finish, on the road or the water.

Pick one service or combine them. Mobile service is available across Orlando, so the work can happen at your home, your office or the dock.

Premium detailing packages

Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, glass, and a full interior clean with seats and carpet shampooed. Built as packages so you know what's included before you book.

Polishing and paint correction

One- and multi-step machine polishing to remove swirls, haze, water spots and light scratches, so the paint looks clean even in full Florida sun.

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer that locks in the gloss after correction and shrugs off sun, rain spots, bird droppings and love bugs. Washes get easier for years.

Marine detailing

Oxidized gelcoat polished back to a shine, hull and deck washed and sealed, vinyl and interiors cleaned. Done at the dock or on the trailer.

About
