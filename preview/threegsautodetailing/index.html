Services

Everything a detail center should do, in one bay.

Book one service or stack them. Bring the car to the shop in St. Augustine and pick it up finished.

Showroom interior and exterior detail

Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, glass, and a deep interior clean with steam on seats, carpet and vents. Built to look like it just rolled off the lot.

Paint correction and scratch removal

Machine polishing to remove swirls, haze, water spots and light scratches, so the paint reads clean even in full St. Augustine sun.

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer that locks in the gloss after correction and shrugs off salt air, sun, rain spots and love bugs. Washes get easier for years.

Window tinting

Film cut and installed in-shop to knock down heat and glare and keep the interior from baking. Shades to match your look and Florida's rules.

Steam cleaning and ozone odor removal

Steam lifts grime out of seams and fabric without soaking the cabin. Ozone treatment neutralizes smoke, mildew and pet smells at the source.

Headlight restoration

Yellowed, hazy lenses sanded, polished and sealed so you can actually see at night and the front end looks years newer.
