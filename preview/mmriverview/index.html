Services

What gets fixed on site

Most repairs a shop would do on a lift can be done right where the car sits. If a job really needs a shop, you hear that up front, not after.

Diagnostics

Check engine lights, no-starts, stalls and strange noises. Scanned and road-tested on the spot, then explained in plain English.

A/C repair

Warm air in a Florida summer is not an option. Leak checks, recharges, compressors and blower problems.

Wheel bearings and hubs

That hum that gets louder with speed. Bearings and hub assemblies replaced in your driveway, front or rear.

Engine and cooling

Water pumps, thermostats, belts, hoses, leaks and overheating. Parts priced to your exact vehicle.

Battery, starter, alternator

Car won't crank or dies while driving. Tested on site and replaced without a tow.

General mobile repair

Brakes, oil changes, suspension and the odd jobs that pile up. Ask about anything, cars and trucks alike.
