Services

What Ivan handles on-site

Most of what a shop puts on a lift can be done where the car sits. If a job really does need a bay, you'll hear that up front, not after.

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers and brake lines, front and rear. Grinding, pulsing or a soft pedal sorted in your driveway.

Suspension

Shocks, struts, control arms, tie rods and the clunk over speed bumps. Replaced on the spot, often the same day you call.

Cooling system

Radiators, water pumps, thermostats and fan clutches. Overheating traced to the cause, not just topped off.

Diagnostics

Scanner on, vacuum leaks and warning lights traced methodically, then explained in plain English before anything is replaced.

Oil and fluids

Oil changes, coolant, brake fluid and the maintenance that keeps the big repairs away. Done at your door.

General repair

Starters, alternators, belts, hoses, no-starts and the odd jobs. If it's on the car, ask and you'll get a straight answer.
