What Billy and the crew do

Every car gets the hands-on treatment. No drive-through, no shortcuts, nothing pushed on you that you don't need.

Full detail

Inside and out. Seats, carpets, vents, door jambs, wheels, tires and trim. The nooks and crannies most places skip.

Interior deep clean

Shampoo, extraction, odor removal. Smoke, pets, spilled coffee. Cars that sat for months come back fresh.

Paint correction

Clay bar, multi-stage polish and color correction to pull swirls, oxidation and light scratches out of the paint.

Ceramic coating

Long-term protection on paint and glass. Easier washes, deeper gloss, real resistance to Florida sun.

Headlight restoration

Yellowed, hazy lenses de-oxidized and sealed so you can actually see at night.

Engine bay and trim

Engine bay detailed, faded plastic re-blacked, chrome and glass polished. The finishing touches.
