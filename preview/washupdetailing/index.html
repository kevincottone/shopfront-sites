Services

Everything a detail shop does, minus the shop.

Pick a level or mix and match. Juan walks the car first so you only pay for what it actually needs, and nothing leaves your driveway.

Detail packages, Levels 1–3

Level 1 is a thorough wash and vacuum. Level 2 adds the deep interior. Level 3 is the full reset: clay, polish, shampoo and protection on every surface.

Interior-only detail

Seats, carpets, mats, door panels, vents, cup holders and glass. Crumbs, sand and the dog's contribution all gone, with the cabin wiped and conditioned.

Exterior-only detail

Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, bugs and tar off the front, then a sealant so the Island salt air has something to beat on besides your paint.

Seat & carpet shampoo

Hot-water extraction on cloth seats, carpets and mats. Spills, stains and that smell you've stopped noticing, pulled out instead of covered up.

Engine bay detail

Degreased, rinsed carefully and dressed so the bay looks like the car is for sale. Great before a trade-in or when a mechanic has to go looking for a leak.

Decal removal & mobile wash

Old business lettering, dealer badges and stickers lifted without ghosting. Or just a proper mobile wash between details to keep the car looking fresh.
