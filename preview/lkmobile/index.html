Services

What we handle on-site

From diagnostics and drivability issues to heavy-line work. If it's bigger than a driveway job, we'll tell you up front.

Engine repair

Misfires, check-engine lights, timing, leaks and no-start problems. Diagnosed with a scanner, not a guess.

Brake services

Pads, rotors, calipers and brake lines. Usually finished in your driveway in a couple of hours.

Transmissions

Service, repair and full replacements, including on work trucks and diesels. Done on-site.

Air conditioning

Warm air in a Florida July is an emergency. Leak checks, recharges, compressors and blend doors.

Electrical system

Batteries, starters, alternators, wiring gremlins and dash warning lights, traced to the real cause.

Suspension repair

Struts, shocks, control arms, ball joints and sway bar links. Fixes the clunks and the wander.
