What we do

Two specialties, one shop.

Come in for tint and leave with a sound system too, or the other way around. Electronics round it out so the whole car gets done in one place.

Window tint

Cars, trucks and SUVs, cut to fit your glass. We'll walk you through the shades, keep the front windows within Florida law, and match factory privacy glass in the back.

  • Full car, two fronts or windshield
  • Carbon and ceramic film options
  • Old, purple or bubbled tint removed
  • Most cars done the same day

Car audio

From a step up over the factory system to a full amp-and-sub build with real bass. Clean wiring, a setup that fits how you drive, and a fair rate.

  • Head units and touchscreen receivers
  • Speaker upgrades and component sets
  • Amplifiers, subwoofers and enclosures
  • Daily-driver builds to full-on bass

Electronics

Backup cameras, dash cams, remote start and the other add-ons that make an older car feel new. Ask about adding one to your tint or audio visit.
