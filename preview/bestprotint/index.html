What we do

Tint, protection and graphics under one roof.

Daily drivers, new cars straight off the lot, and whole work fleets. Bring it in, tell Belal what you want it to look like and how long you plan to keep it, and the shop will put together a package that makes sense.

Window tint

All sides and rear, or just the two fronts to match factory glass. Belal explains the shade percentages up front so you pick one you'll be happy with and that stays Florida-legal.

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer over the paint that makes washing easier, keeps water beading and holds the gloss through a Florida summer.

Paint protection film

Clear film on the bumper, hood, fenders and mirrors, the spots that catch I-4 rock chips first. Invisible on the car, easy to live with.

Wraps and color change

Full color-change wraps, satin and matte finishes, roofs, hoods and chrome delete. A new look without touching the factory paint.

Fleet graphics

Logos, lettering, phone numbers and partial or full wraps for work trucks, vans and trailers. One van or the whole fleet, kept consistent.

Signs, printing and chip repair

Banners, storefront signs and printed graphics for your business, plus windshield chip repair done while your car is in for tint.

Bundle and save a trip. Tint, PPF and ceramic coating are often done together on a new car. Ask about doing them in one visit.
