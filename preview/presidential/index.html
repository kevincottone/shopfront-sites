Services

Four things, done properly.

Every car is walked through before it's quoted, so you only pay for what your paint actually needs. Combine services and the car stays in one bay from start to finish.

Paint protection film (PPF)

Clear, self-healing film over the front end or the whole car. Rock chips, bug etching and road debris hit the film instead of your paint. Partial front, full front or full body.

Ceramic coating

A hard, glossy layer bonded to the paint, wheels and glass. Water sheets off, washes take minutes, and the metallic in your paint finally shows. Annual booster visits keep it fresh.

Window tint

Heat-rejecting tint that keeps the cabin cooler in a Tampa summer and cuts glare without the purple fade. Legal shades for every window, cut cleanly to the glass.

Paint correction and detailing

Machine polishing that removes swirls, water spots and dealer-installed scratches, plus full interior and exterior detailing. The step that makes everything else look right.
