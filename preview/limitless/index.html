Services

Everything from a maintenance wash to a ceramic coat.

Pick one service or stack them. Every visit is fully mobile, so there's nothing to drop off and nowhere to wait.

Interior detailing

Shampooed seats and carpet, leather cleaned and conditioned, vents, cup holders, pet hair and stains. Every surface wiped down and protected.

Exterior detailing

Hand wash and dry, wheels and tires cleaned and dressed, door jambs, glass, and a sealant so water beads for weeks under the Florida sun.

Paint correction

Machine polishing that lifts swirls, water spots and light scratches out of the clear coat. The step that makes everything on top of it look right.

Ceramic coating

A hard, glossy layer bonded to the paint for long-term protection against sun, rain and love bugs. Water sheets off and washes take minutes.

Maintenance plans

Weekly, bi-weekly or monthly visits at a lower per-visit rate. Some customers have Chad out every week; the car never gets far from that just-detailed look.

Trucks, SUVs and multiple cars

Four-door trucks and large SUVs welcome. Two or more vehicles at the same address can be done in one visit.
