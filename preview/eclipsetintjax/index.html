What we do

One shop for every pane of glass

Eclipse Window Tinting covers the car in the driveway, the windows behind it and the storefront downtown. Tell Bryan what you've got and he'll recommend a film for how you actually use it.

Automotive window tint

All side windows plus the rear glass on cars, trucks and SUVs. Carbon for a clean black look, ceramic when you want the Jacksonville heat out of the cabin. Fronts kept Florida-legal.

Residential tint

Cut the heat and glare on west-facing rooms, sliders and lanai doors, protect floors and furniture from fading, and keep the view out.

Commercial tint

Storefronts, offices and conference rooms. Lower cooling bills, less glare on screens, and a more even look from the street.

Security film

Thick, clear film that holds shattered glass together through a break-in attempt or a storm, on homes and businesses.

Clear bra paint protection

Clear film on the hood, bumper and mirrors so the gravel on I-95 and I-295 doesn't leave its mark on your paint.

Tint removal & replacement

Purple, bubbling film stripped clean, adhesive off, glass prepped, then new film laid right.

Quoted straight, no upsell

Reviews mention it more than once: Bryan tells you what the job needs and doesn't push extras. Text your car or your window count and get a real number back.
