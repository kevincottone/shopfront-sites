What we do

Tint that keeps the heat out

Every vehicle is cut and installed by Dexter himself. Pick a shade, or tell him how you drive and let him recommend one.

Full vehicle tint

All side windows plus the rear glass, shade matched all the way around. Cars, trucks, SUVs and Broncos alike.

Two front windows

Factory privacy glass in back already? Match the two fronts so the whole car reads as one, usually in under an hour.

UV protection film

Clear or light film for the front windows and windshield that blocks UV and cuts cabin heat without darkening your view.

Windshield strip

A tinted band across the top of the windshield to knock down the low-sun glare on 56 and I-75 in the morning.

Wait in comfort. Customers keep mentioning the waiting area: a sofa, snacks and drinks, and a job that's done faster than they expected.
