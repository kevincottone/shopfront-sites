Services

Protect it, then keep it that way.

Every car gets a free estimate before anything is quoted. Combine ceramic, film and detailing and it all happens in one visit, under one roof in Oldsmar.

Ceramic coating

A hard, glossy layer bonded to the paint, and optionally wheels and glass. Water sheets off, bugs and bird droppings wipe away, and the color looks deeper than the day you bought it.

Paint protection film (PPF)

Clear, self-healing film over the hood, fenders, bumper and grille, or the whole car. Rock chips and road debris hit the film instead of your paint. Works on matte finishes too.

Paint correction

Machine polishing that lifts swirls, water spots and light scratches out of the clear coat before any coating goes on. The step that makes everything on top of it look right.

Interior and exterior detailing

Seats and carpet shampooed, leather cleaned and conditioned, every vent and crevice. Ten-year-old trucks come back looking brand new inside and out.
