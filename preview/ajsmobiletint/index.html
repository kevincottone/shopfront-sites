What we do

Window tint for everything with glass.

Most jobs are cars and trucks, but AJ also tints RVs, boats, homes and storefronts. Tell him what you have and what you want out of it, less heat, more privacy, or a cleaner look, and he'll walk you through the options.

Mobile automotive tint

Sedans, coupes, SUVs, trucks and vans, tinted in your driveway or parking lot. All sides and rear, or just the two fronts to match factory glass.

Nano ceramic film

Blocks infrared heat instead of just light, so the cabin stays cooler even at a lighter, Florida-legal shade. No metal, no signal interference.

Windshield tint

A clear or light heat film across the windshield, or a visor strip along the top, to cut the glare on Highway 50 and the Turnpike.

Headlight and tail light tint

A smoked or light tint on the lenses for a sharper, more uniform look. Ask AJ about shades that keep the lights fully visible.

Home and office windows

Residential and commercial film for sliders, big west-facing windows and storefronts. Lower AC bills, less fading, and more privacy in the daytime.

RV and boat tint

Motorhomes, travel trailers and boat cabins get hot fast on a Florida lake or campground. Tinted at the storage lot, the marina or your driveway.

No shop visit needed. AJ works out of a fully equipped van, so the job gets done at your place, on your schedule.
