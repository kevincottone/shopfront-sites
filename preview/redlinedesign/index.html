The shop

Design and install under one roof

Redline Design is a wrap and protection shop on Massaro Blvd in Tampa, run by Adam. Customers come in for a full color change on a classic E30, PPF and tint on a brand-new Bronco, or stripes and a custom decal on a Durango, and they leave with work other shops send their own customers here to see.

Everything starts with a conversation about the car and the look you're after. You get a firm price, a date, and a straight answer about materials. Then it gets installed by the same crew that quoted it.
